Solve small crosswords using only the letter tiles you draw. Every tile scores points, every finished word adds mult, and each play scores points × mult.

How it plays

Beat each round's target, then spend your earnings in the shop on upgraded tiles, charms that bend the rules and one-shot tools. Lean into a build (money, growing tiles, discards, long words, gem upgrades) and watch the multipliers stack into huge combos.

  • 20-round runs, with an editor round every third round and the Editor-in-Chief at round 20
  • 120 charms across 9 builds, 41 tile upgrades and 20 tools
  • 12 tile sets, each unlocked by winning a run with the one before and each a step harder
  • A daily run: the same 10 rounds for everyone each day, with a result card to share
  • 50 achievements that unlock new charms, tiles and tools
  • English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, German and Russian, each with its own words and letter bag
  • No account needed. Rewarded ads are optional
